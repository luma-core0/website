Some statement about us

LUMA CORE SDN. BHD is responsible for planning, installing, testing, repairing, and maintaining electrical systems. The company works with electrical cables, switches, sockets, lighting, distribution boards, circuit protection equipment, and other electrical fittings.The main objective is to make sure electricity is distributed safely, reliably, and efficiently throughout a property.Our main services is Electrical wiring,Lighting installation,Switches & sockets,Distribution boards,Electrical fittings,Electrical installation,Electrical troubleshooting,Maintenance & repairs and last but not least renovation and rewiring.Proper electrical installation helps reduce the risk of electric shock, electrical fires, equipment damage, power interruptions, and overloaded circuits. Good workmanship also helps ensure that the electrical system operates efficiently and can support the customer's current and future power requirements.We strive to provide electrical installation services that are safe, dependable, and completed to a high standard, while maintaining customer satisfaction on every project.
